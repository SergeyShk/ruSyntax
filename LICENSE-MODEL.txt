The model of ruSyntax (data/ru-syntax.bin) and the check file of its forward pass (data/check.conllu)
are made from the training and development parts of the Universal Dependencies treebanks, release
2.19 (https://universaldependencies.org), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):

- UD_Russian-Taiga (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-Taiga)
  by Olga Lyashevskaya, Olga Rudina, Natalia Vlasova and Anna Zhuravleva;
- UD_Russian-GSD (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-GSD),
  annotated and converted by Google, by Ryan McDonald, Vitaly Nikolaev and
  Olga Lyashevskaya;
- UD_Russian-Poetry (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Russian-Poetry)
  by Olga Lyashevskaya, Natalia Vlasova and Dmitri Sitchinava.

The part-of-speech tags and features the model is trained on are given by ruMorph
(https://github.com/SergeyShk/ruMorph), whose dictionary is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The ruSyntax model is an adaptation of that data and is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
